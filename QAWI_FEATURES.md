# Qawi — Built Features Inventory

*A complete list of customer- and business-facing features already implemented in the Qawi Gym Management Platform. For investors, gym owners, and operators.*

---

## Dashboard & Overview

### Role-Based Dashboards
Every user type gets a tailored home screen: Gym Admin sees KPIs and member counts; Coach sees assigned members with nutrition status; Reception sees live check-ins; Finance sees revenue; HR sees attendance; Members see today's workout and check-in stats.

### Real-Time Activity Feed
Reception and admin dashboards display live check-in events, walk-in registrations, and subscription renewal requests as they happen.

---

## Member Management

### Member Profiles
Store complete member information including contact details, subscription status, coach assignment, self-training eligibility, and account activity history in one centralized view.

### Member CRUD
Add new members, update profiles, deactivate or delete accounts, reset passwords, and assign or reassign coaches from a single interface.

### Walk-In Registration
Reception staff can instantly register walk-in visitors and convert them to full gym members without leaving the check-in flow.

### Coach Assignment
Link members to specific coaches for personalized training and nutrition oversight. Coaches can only access their assigned members' data.

### Self-Training Mode
Enable selected members to create their own workout plans and set nutrition targets independently, reducing coach workload for experienced gym-goers.

### Subscription Status Tracking
Visual indicators show which members are active, expiring soon, or expired, with direct renewal request workflows.

---

## Membership & Subscription Management

### Subscription Plan Management
Gym admins define membership tiers (e.g., 1-month, 3-month, 6-month, 12-month) with custom pricing, duration, and feature access.

### Renewal Requests
Members can submit subscription renewal requests through the mobile app or web portal. Admins and receptionists process them with one-click approval.

### Expiring Subscription Alerts
Automatic alerts highlight members whose subscriptions expire within the week, enabling proactive retention outreach.

### Renewal History
Track the full subscription lifecycle for every member, including plan changes, renewals, and historical pricing.

---

## Coach Management

### Coach Dashboard
Coaches see all assigned members at a glance, with color-coded nutrition status dots (green = on track, red = missing, gray = not set) and last-visit tracking to identify disengaged clients.

### Coach Assignment & Portfolio
Admins assign coaches to members. Coaches manage their full portfolio of clients from a dedicated dashboard.

### Coach Performance Metrics
HR and gym admins can review coach performance through member engagement and assignment statistics.

---

## Workout Management

### Custom Workout Plan Builder
Coaches create structured multi-day workout plans for individual members, specifying exercises, sets, reps, and rest periods for each training day.

### Workout Templates
Save commonly used workout structures as reusable templates (e.g., "12-Week Strength," "Fat Loss Program") to dramatically reduce planning time.

### Exercise Library
A searchable, bilingual (English/Arabic) database of 70+ exercises across 7 categories, each with video guidance. Gyms can override default names and videos to match their equipment.

### Workout Completion Tracking
Members mark exercises and full days as complete. Coaches and members see real-time adherence and progress.

### Today's Workout View
Members open the app and immediately see exactly what exercises they need to perform today, with set/rep targets and video demos.

### Upcoming Schedule Preview
Members can browse their full weekly or monthly workout schedule to plan around rest days and intensity variations.

---

## Nutrition Management

### Nutrition Target Setting
Coaches and nutritionists set personalized daily calorie and macro targets (protein, carbs, fats) for each member based on their goals.

### Daily Food Logging
Members log meals throughout the day with a searchable food database, including 25+ Arabic and Middle Eastern foods pre-loaded. The app calculates remaining calories and macros in real time.

### Macro Progress Visualization
Visual calorie ring and macro breakdown bars show members exactly how much of each nutrient they've consumed versus their daily target.

### Water Intake Tracker
Members log water consumption in milliliters or liters, tracking daily hydration progress against their goal.

### Meal Suggestions
The system suggests meals and recipes aligned with the member's nutrition targets and dietary preferences.

### Nutrition Calendar Plans
Coaches can build week-by-week or month-by-month nutrition plans, assigning specific daily targets and meal structures in advance.

### AI-Powered Food Analysis (V3)
Members describe what they ate in plain text, and AI automatically extracts calories, protein, carbs, and fats — no manual searching required.

### Nutrition Workflow Modes
Gyms configure whether nutrition management is handled by coaches only, nutritionists only, or both, matching the gym's staffing model.

## Body Measurements & Progress Tracking

### Body Measurement Logging
Record and track weight, body fat percentage, chest, waist, arms, legs, and other key measurements over time.

### Measurement History
View complete historical graphs and tables of every measurement taken, enabling coaches and members to visualize physical transformation trends.

### Personal Records (PRs) Tracking
Members see their all-time best weights and reps for each exercise, celebrating strength milestones and motivating continued progress.

### Workout History Review
Browse past training sessions with full exercise-by-exercise detail, including sets, reps, weights, and completion notes.

## Attendance & Check-In

### Mobile Check-In/Check-Out
Members tap a button in the mobile app to mark their gym attendance. Offline mode queues check-ins when connectivity is poor and syncs automatically.

### Reception Manual Check-In
Receptionists search for members by name and manually mark them present, with a live feed showing everyone currently in the gym.

### Today's Attendance Stats
See total members checked in today, current occupancy, and check-in trends at a glance.

### Attendance Streak Tracking
Members see their consecutive check-in streak, gamifying gym consistency and encouraging regular visits.

### Biometric Device Integration (ZkTeco)
Register and sync ZkTeco fingerprint/face recognition devices. Members enroll biometrically and check in by scanning at the front desk.
